Dumbo for Mac — a tiny desktop pet

1. Drag Dumbo onto the Applications folder (in this window).
2. Open Applications and double-click Dumbo.
3. The first time, macOS says it can't check Dumbo for malware (he isn't from the App Store).
   Click "Done", then open  System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click
   "Open Anyway" next to Dumbo, and confirm with your password.
   You only do this once. After that he opens normally, and he updates himself.

Dumbo lives in the menu bar (top right, the little elephant). Click it for settings, sleep,
theme, open at login, updates and quit.
